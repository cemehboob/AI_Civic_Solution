Role: You are a requirements analyst and technical architect specializing in ICT4D, with deep experience in South Asian rural contexts (low literacy, intermittent internet, multilingual coexistence).

Project Background:
We are building a "Voice-First E-Governance" platform for rural and remote areas of Pakistan (basic internet available). Target users are semi-literate or illiterate, speaking Urdu, Punjabi, and potentially other local languages. Users should report complaints, check hospital information, and inquire about subsidies through natural speech—no menu navigation, no text input.

Constraints:
1. Lightweight frontend (mobile + web), suitable for low-end Android and slow networks.
2. Lightweight backend.
3. AI capabilities must use free models only.
4. When a free model's tokens are exhausted, the system must switch to another free model—no paid fallback.
5. Must support Urdu and Punjabi (ASR + TTS).

Verified Technical Facts (must be reflected in your recommendations):
- OpenRouter's openrouter/free router automatically selects among free models but only falls back to other free models. If paid models are added to the fallback array, they will be billed.
- OpenRouter free models have rate limits: accounts without any top-up history are limited to 50 requests/day; after purchasing $10 in credits once, the limit increases to 1,000 requests/day.
- Shruti-ONNX supports Urdu and Punjabi, with an int8 version requiring approximately 1GB of memory, suitable for offline deployment.
- Libra-TTS supports Urdu and Punjabi, has an ONNX version that can run on CPU, and is suitable for lightweight deployment.
- Whisper supports Urdu and Punjabi, but zero-shot performance on low-resource languages is limited; fine-tuning can significantly reduce WER.

Please deliver:
A. Functional requirements list for mobile and web (with emphasis on voice interaction flow for illiterate users)
B. Recommended lightweight frontend tech stack (with justification)
C. Recommended lightweight backend tech stack (with justification)
D. Free AI model selection for ASR, TTS, and LLM (Urdu/Punjabi support), with a concrete model-switching architecture design. The architecture must address the fact that OpenRouter free models have a daily request cap.
E. Key risks and mitigation
